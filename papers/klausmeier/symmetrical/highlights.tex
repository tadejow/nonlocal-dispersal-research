\section*{Highlights}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Well-posedness of the non-local Klausmeier vegetation model is established.
  \item A critical patch size threshold for vegetation survival is studied.
  \item A minimum biomass density required to prevent ecosystem collapse analytically determined.
  \item Fat-tailed kernels enhance resilience, allowing survival in smaller habitats.
  \item Non-local interactions support stable patterns with sharp biomass gradients.
\end{itemize}
