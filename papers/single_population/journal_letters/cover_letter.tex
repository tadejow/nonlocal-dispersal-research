I am writing to submit for publication my manuscript titled ``Long time behaviour of solutions to non-local and non-linear dispersal problems'' in the Journal of Differential Equations.

This work contributes to the spectral analysis of so called dispersal operators imitating Neumann boundary conditions and application to the theory of asymptotic behaviour and stationary solutions. Namely we show a criterion for a convergence to the mean mass and derive existence of both continuous and discontinuous steady states. Moreover we are able to show the stability of obtained discontinuous steady state

Given the relevance and novelty of the research presented, I believe that it aligns well with the scope and standards of the Journal of Differential Equations. I am confident that this manuscript will be of interest to the journal's readership and will make a valuable addition to its scholarly contributions.

Thank you for considering my submission. I eagerly anticipate your feedback and the opportunity to contribute to the esteemed body of work published in the Journal of Differential Equations.
