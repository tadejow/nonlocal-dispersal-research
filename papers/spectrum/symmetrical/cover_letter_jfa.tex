I am pleased to submit the research article entitled \textbf{“Eigenvalues and eigenfunctions of the non-local dispersal with Neumann-type boundary condition and symmetric kernel”} for consideration for publication in the \textit{Journal of Functional Analysis}.

This study addresses a fundamental gap in the spectral theory of non-local dispersal operators on bounded domains. Unlike the classical Laplacian, these integral operators lack compactness, which gives rise to an essential spectrum and complicates the study of the discrete spectrum. While the non-local Dirichlet problem is relatively well-understood, the existence of non-trivial eigenfunctions for the non-local Neumann boundary condition on arbitrary domains has remained an open problem.

In this manuscript, we provide a rigorous variational construction of a sequence of non-trivial eigenfunctions corresponding to isolated eigenvalues located strictly above the continuous spectrum. The main novelty of our work is two-fold:
\begin{itemize}
    \item We establish explicit criteria linking the domain's size and shape with the variance of the dispersal kernel, guaranteeing the existence of the spectral gap.
    \item We develop a finite-dimensional Galerkin approximation framework and rigorously prove the strong $L^2$-convergence of the minimizing sequences to the exact eigenpairs by decomposing the operator into compact and multiplication parts.
\end{itemize}

The established variational framework provides a much-needed theoretical foundation for the stability analysis of non-local reaction-diffusion models. We believe our methodology and results are a perfect fit for the \textit{Journal of Functional Analysis}.

This manuscript has not been published previously, is not under consideration for publication elsewhere, and its publication is approved by the author.
