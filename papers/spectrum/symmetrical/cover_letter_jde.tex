\noindent
I am pleased to submit the manuscript entitled \textbf{"Eigenvalues and eigenfunctions of the non-local dispersal with Neumann-type boundary condition and symmetric kernel"} for consideration for publication in the \textit{Journal of Differential Equations}.

This study solves an open problem in the spectral theory of non-local dispersal operators on bounded domains. Unlike the classical Laplacian, these integral operators lack compactness. This creates an essential spectrum and makes the study of discrete eigenvalues very difficult. While the non-local Dirichlet problem is understood, finding non-trivial eigenfunctions for the non-local Neumann boundary condition on bounded domains remained unsolved.

In this paper, we provide a rigorous variational construction of a sequence of non-trivial eigenfunctions. The main novelties of our work are:
\begin{itemize}
    \item We define explicit geometric conditions linking the domain's size and shape with the kernel's variance. This guarantees the existence of the spectral gap.
    \item We construct a finite-dimensional Galerkin approximation and rigorously prove the strong $L^2$-convergence of the minimizing sequences to the exact eigenpairs.
\end{itemize}

Our mathematical framework provides a necessary theoretical foundation for the stability analysis of non-local reaction-diffusion equations. Therefore, we believe our results fit perfectly within the scope of the \textit{Journal of Differential Equations}.

This manuscript has not been published previously and is not under consideration for publication elsewhere. All authors approve its submission.
